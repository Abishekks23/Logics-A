About Us

✿ From Students to Professionals. From Ideas to Businesses.

Logics’A is a professional platform dedicated to Finance, Accounts, Audit, Taxation, Education, Career Guidance, and Business Consultancy. We provide end-to-end support for individuals, students, freelancers, entrepreneurs, and businesses.

Our services include GST, IT filing, TDS, accounting, audit, and other compliance-related services, along with specialized coaching for 11th & 12th Accountancy (State & CBSE), CA, CS, and CMA professional students. We also guide aspiring professionals through practical training and article-ship opportunities.

For students who have completed their 12th standard, Logics’A provides career counselling, job guidance, skill development, and training in Accounts, Finance, and Audit, helping them choose the right career path and become industry-ready.

We also support freelancers and new business owners by helping them build practical knowledge and skills in accounting, finance, audit, business systems, and financial management—enabling them to confidently manage and grow their own businesses.
