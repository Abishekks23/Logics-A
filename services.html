Income Tax Returns (ITR-1) Guide

1 Basic Requisite for ITR 1

  • PAN card
  • Aadhaar card (Must be linked with PAN)
  • Salary slip / Form 16 (From employer)
  • Bank account (Active + linked to PAN)
  • Form 26AS / AIS

2 Create/Login Income Tax Account

3 Check Profile (IMPORTANT)

  • Go to My Profile and check Personal details
  • Bank account (must be pre-validated)
  • Aadhaar linked status
  • If bank not added: Add bank → verify via OTP → wait for validation

4 File ITR

  • Click e-File → Select Income Tax Returns → Click File Income Tax Return → ITR 1 (Sahaj)

5 Enter Income Details & Deductions

  • Check auto-filled Salary income, Bank interest, TDS
  • Enter any missing details for Salary, House Property, or Other Income
  • 80C: LIC, PPF, ELSS, tuition fees
  • 80D: Health insurance
  • 80TTA: Savings account interest (max ₹10,000)

6 Tax Calculation & Pay Tax

  • Click "Preview Return" to see Total income and Tax payable/refund
  • Pay the tax and click Submit.
Your return is filed but NOT completed yet! You must E-Verify.

7 E-Verify

  • Choose a method: Aadhaar OTP (fastest), Net banking, or EVC.
  • Enter OTP → Confirm
  • You will get an Acknowledgement number (ITR-V). Download the PDF.
🌿 Here now, your ITR return is Filed and verified.
