What is ITR-1?

ITR-1 (Sahaj) is an Income Tax Return form used by individual taxpayers in India who have simple income sources.

It is the most basic and commonly used ITR form.

✿

Why is ITR-1 filed?

ITR-1 is filed to:

  • Report your income to the Income Tax Department
  • Calculate your final tax liability
  • Claim refund if extra tax is paid (TDS/excess deduction)

[ Practical hint – Yes, In most of the cases people usually wants filing ITR for the purpose of getting REFUND of our taxes ]

✿

Who should file ITR-1?

You can file ITR-1 if you are an Individual Resident (not HUF/company) and have:

Allowed income sources:

  • Salary or pension income
  • Income from one house property
  • Other sources like:
    • Bank interest
    • Fixed deposit interest
    • Savings account interest
✿
